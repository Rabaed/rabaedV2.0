Submittal panel — anatomy

Click a row in List view or a card in Kanban to open this panel from the right. Pick a size in the top bar or drag the edge to any width; the layout inside changes with the width. Headers such as Subject, Scope and Location fold to one line so the panel stays short.

Compact · 440pxOne pane, stacked fields, key facts 2×2. Prev/next hidden.
Normal · 640px (default)One pane, 2-column fields, 4 key facts in a row.
Wide · 960pxTwo panes. Side rail: workflow, people, latest activity.
Full · to the sidebarTwo panes, 3-column fields, rail 360px. Works like a page.

1 · Compact 440px

Numbered outlines + legend show every part of the panel.

2 · Normal 640px

Default size. Revisions show after the number — TWR-TMC-CV-MAR-280 Rev 1.

3 · Wide 960px

Side rail appears. The Workflow section moves out of the main column into the rail.

4 · Full to the sidebar

Covers the list. Fields go to three columns. Drag the edge to shrink back.

5 · Sections collapsed Normal

Every header folded. Each shows a one-line summary — Subject title, Scope trade, Location path, item and file counts.

7 · Workflow template click Workflow

Press the route strip or “Workflow template” to see every step: party, roles, company and time allowed.

8 · Internal communication Wide

A timeline like the approval log — internal returns, notes and re-sends. Only your company sees it.

9 · Attachment viewer in app

Press the eye button (or the file row) to preview the PDF or photo right under the file — page thumbnails, zoom, print, download. No pop-up. ↗ opens it large inside the panel.

6 · From Kanban Wide

Same panel from the board. The open card is outlined so you keep your place.
