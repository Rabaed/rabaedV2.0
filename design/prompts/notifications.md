# Design request: Notifications, the bell and the page (RP-416)

Paste the shared brief from `design-requests-2026-10-07.md` first.

**What it is:** in-app notifications. The bell in the top bar shows the unread count and a short dropdown; "View all" opens the full Notifications page.

**Who uses it:** everyone. Example viewers: Ali (TMC Project Manager) and Mohammed (Consultant manager).

## Content

- Notification kinds (each row: what happened, Document Number left-to-right · Subject, Project, time, unread dot):
  - "Reached you at <Step>" (a Step reached me or my Step Pool).
  - From items I watch: a Transition, a Review Code or Inspection Result, a new Revision, Cancelled.
  - "Sent Back to you by <Company>".
  - "Vacancy at <Step>: assign a replacement" (for the Authorized Person).
- Another Company is named by its name only; never its people, its internal Steps or its Recommended Codes.
- Actions: open the item (marks the row read), **Mark all as read**, filter Unread / All, and per-Project grouping or a Project filter.
- The bell dropdown: the latest 5–8, "Mark all as read", "View all", link to Notification settings.
- A "Step reached" notification disappears when a colleague claims the item.
- Empty state ("No notifications yet").

## Frames

1. Bell dropdown open (desktop), 3 unread.
2. Full page as Ali, a mix of kinds, Unread filter on.
