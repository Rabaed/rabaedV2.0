# Design request: Sign-in and accept invitation (RP-420)

Paste the shared brief from `design-requests-2026-10-07.md` first. These pages sit **outside** the app shell.

## Pages

1. **Welcome** (`/`, signed out): Rabaed logo, one line on what Rabaed is (construction document control for KSA projects), Sign in.
2. **Sign in:** Email, Password, Sign in; errors "That email and password don't match." and "Something went wrong. Try again."; the language switch (العربية / English).
3. **Accept your invitation:** a Member invited by their Company's Authorized Person sets a password (at least the minimum length, shown as a hint), confirms it, and is signed in. Show an expired or used invitation.

No sign-up page (Companies are onboarded by Rabaed; Members are invited), no social sign-in.

## Frames

Each page on desktop and phone (402), English and Arabic, plus each error state.
