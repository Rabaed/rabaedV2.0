# Design request: Activity page (RP-421)

Paste the shared brief from `design-requests-2026-10-07.md` first. The Dashboard's Activity Feed panel (`views-dashboard-notifications.md` §5) opens this page with "View all".

**What it is:** the full Activity Feed of one Project: what happened on its Work Items, as the viewer may see it.

## Content

- Each entry: **<Company>** <did what> **<Document Number>** · Subject, and the time. Names of people only for the viewer's own Company. Another Company is one name, never its people, internal Steps, Internal Notes or Recommended Codes.
- The viewer's own internal events are marked "Only your Company sees this".
- Filters: Module, Type, "Only items I'm on"; group by day (Today, Yesterday, dates).
- Loads more on scroll; each entry opens its item.
- Not in the feed: answer changes and Document uploads.
