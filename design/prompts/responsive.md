# Design request: tablet and phone layouts (RP-422)

Paste the shared brief from `design-requests-2026-10-07.md` first.

**What it is:** how the app's shell and main templates look on a **tablet (iPad Air 11" portrait, 820 × 1180)** and a **phone (iPhone Pro, about 402 × 874)**, in English and Arabic. Desktop is the kit as it is. Until these designs exist, the build only guarantees that nothing breaks: no page scrolls sideways, tables and the Kanban scroll inside their own area, and the sidebar opens as a drawer.

## Screens to show at 820 and 402

1. **Shell:** how the sidebar opens and closes (drawer from a menu button), the top bar (bell, Member), the project header and tabs (scrolling tabs or a menu).
2. **Home:** count tiles, Needs my action, Recent activity, your Projects.
3. **Projects:** the card grid.
4. **Submittals List:** the toolbar (search, Filter, Need My Action), the filter panel as a sheet, and the table: decide between scrolling sideways with the Document Number pinned, or one card per row.
5. **Kanban:** columns scroll left and right (snapping to each column), each column scrolls up and down; how dragging works by touch (or a "Move to…" action instead).
6. **Project Dashboard:** Type cards stacked, the Activity panel below.
7. **Settings layout:** the settings nav as a select or a sheet, section cards stacked.

Touch targets at least 44 × 44. Arabic mirrored throughout.
