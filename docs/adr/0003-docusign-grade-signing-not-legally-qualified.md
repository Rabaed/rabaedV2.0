# Signatures are technically binding like DocuSign, not legally qualified

Every Member who acts on a Work Item signs it, and the Documental Record must stand as evidence. We decided to match the technical strength of platforms like DocuSign without becoming, or yet integrating, a legally qualified e-signature provider in KSA. Each signing Transition asks the Member to confirm in a dialog (no OTP or password re-entry for now), then records who signed, when, and a hash of the Work Item's exact content at that moment in the append-only audit log. The final PDF is sealed with Rabaed's own digital certificate (PAdES) and a trusted timestamp, carries a verification QR code, and ends with a signing-trail page. A qualified provider, or OTP step-up, can be added later behind the same signing step without changing the Workflow model.
