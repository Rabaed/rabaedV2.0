# RP-1261 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1261 (ADR 0028: a withdrawn Company comes back only through a new Invitation; visibility.md "A Participant's withdrawal"), in their setup: Project "Tower", C1 the Host Company (its Authorized Person is the Project Admin), K1 the Consultant, C2 and C3 Contractors. C2's and C3's items at K1 were Cancelled by their withdrawals, C2's Draft with them; C1's item K1 held became a Participant-level Vacancy by K1's. Then C1's Project Admin invited K1 and C2 again in their roles and both accepted; C3 was not invited again. E2 (Signatory Access) is RP-614's, not built yet, so "plus E2" adds nothing yet.
