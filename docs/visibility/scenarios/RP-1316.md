# RP-1316 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1316: a Work Item gets its Document Number at its first Submit out of its raiser's Participant, not at Send for Review (ADR 0030; owner's decisions on RP-1302; UAT WF-16). They sit beside scenario 53 (V1). Their setup: Project "Tower", C1 the Contractor with an engineer and a PM, K1 the Consultant, an Owner Representative (OR; seam 2: the Owner with oversight), and C2 a second Contractor, all covering every Trade and Location. Seam 1 `apps/api/test/rp-1316-number-at-first-submit.test.ts` (the Rabaed Default MAR); seam 2 `packages/db/test/rp-1316-visibility-rls.test.ts` (the test Send Back Workflow, as the app role).

K1 and OR read the third with the number right after the first's, and every C1 number they read anywhere (the List, the events) runs with no gap; they never read the cancelled item. C2 reads none of C1's items or numbers (V3) |

Nobody outside C1 reads it at all: no item (the plain 404), no List row, no search hit, no event, so no number, count or gap reaches them before its first Submit (V1) |
