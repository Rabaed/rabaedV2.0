# RP-1155 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1155 (the Participants pages to the owner's reference: Company Projects, Project Members, a Member's Visibility). Setup: a Project of C1's (Contractor, its creator and Project Admin), with K1 as its Consultant and C2 as another Contractor; each Company has an Authorized Person and Project Members.

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1155-1 | Company Projects (`GET /v1/participants`) shows each Project's count of Project Members, and Project Members (`GET /v1/participants/:id/members`) shows each Member's Visibility | K1's Authorized Person and K1's other Project Member; C1's Authorized Person; C2's engineer | Each reads their own Company's Participant only: the count is of their own Company's Project Members, and the Members and their Visibility are their own Company's (V15, V16). Nothing in either read points at another Participant (C2 for K1; C1 and K1 for C2): no id, no CR number, no count. Another Company's Project Members list is the 404 that names nothing, to its Project Admin too (seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1155-visibility.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1155-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
| RP-1155-2 | The Visibility Gap: the Trades and Locations a Participant covers that none of its active Project Members do (`gap` on the Project Members read, `app.participant_visibility_gap`). Scenario 33, built | The Participant's Authorized Person; its other Project Members; the Project Admin; another Company's Authorized Person | Only the Participant's own Authorized Person reads it, for that Participant only (V16). Its other Project Members get `null`; the Project Admin and every other Company get nothing (the db function returns no rows; the Members read is the 404 that names nothing). A Participant whose Members cover all of it has an empty gap (seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1155-visibility.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1155-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
| RP-1155-3 | The Authorized Person adds a Member of their Company to the Project (`app.add_project_member`) | The added Member; their Company's other Members; another Company | The new Project Member holds "all" in every dimension: all of their Participant's Visibility and never more (V4). They cover what the Participant covers and nothing else, and their grant is read by their own Company only. Adding them closes the gap they fill (seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1155-visibility.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1155-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
