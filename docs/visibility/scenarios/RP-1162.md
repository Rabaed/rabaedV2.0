# RP-1162 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1162 (Rabaed Admin reassigns a stuck Step, UAT ADM-10; workflow-engine.md §5.6, ADR 0018). Seam 1: a MAR C1 Submitted to K1, whose managers share the pool. Seam 2: a Snag K1 raised on C1, picked up by C1's PM; C2 is another Contractor on the Project.

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1162-1 | A Rabaed Engineer, in Rabaed Admin with a reason, finds the item by Project and Document Number and hands its Step to another Member of the holding Participant's pool, then back to that pool | The holding Participant's Members; the raiser's and every other Company's Members | Only the holding Participant's Members read it: one `admin_reassigned` event internal to them (V5), read as "Reassigned by Rabaed" (or "Assigned to … by Rabaed", "Returned to the pool by Rabaed"), naming no Company and no person as its actor, and never the Engineer: no Member reads `actor_engineer_id`, and no read holds the Engineer's name or email (V14). The new holder gets "Step reached" once; the other pool Members' unread one is withdrawn; back in the pool, each pool Member is told again. Every other Company's reads of the item are exactly as before, at both seams: its history, item, List row, Activity Feed, bell, Step, Stage and Step Age (seam 1: `apps/api/test/admin-reassign-step.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/admin-reassign-step-rls.test.ts`; Rabaed Admin's routes and `admin_action` rows: `apps/admin/test/steps.test.ts`) |
| RP-1162-2 | The Engineer picks another Company's Member (the raiser's, another Contractor's, its Authorized Person), a made-up id, or the holder themself | Every Company's Members | Refused `not_offered`, whoever it names: only the holding Participant's own Step Pool (`app.assignment_pool`) is accepted. Nothing changes: the holder, the events and every Company's reads stay as they were; the refusal is still an `admin_action` with its reason (V9) |
