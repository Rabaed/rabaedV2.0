# RP-625 scenarios

| RP-625-1 | C1's PM drafts a Snag on C1 itself (the Snag's Version 3), raises it (informing the Consultant), moves it In Progress and On Hold, resolves it and closes it | K1 engineer; C2 engineer | K1 reads nothing of the Draft. From the Raise K1 reads the item, its answers, its Documents as they were at the Raise (the Draft's photo, frozen there: nothing is added after, V19, ADR 0020) and its shared moves only (the Raise, the Resolve, the Close; never C1's In Progress or On Hold moves or pick-ups, V5), and reads it Open, its Step Age from the Raise (never from when the Draft was started), until the Resolve, then Resolved (`app.step_as_seen`, V14). K1 holds no Step, is offered nothing, a Transition it tries is refused (409, `not_holder`), and Need My Action never counts it. C2 never reads it (V3). Seam 1 `apps/api/test/informed-participants.test.ts`, seam 2 `packages/db/test/snag-rls.test.ts` |
