# RP-1251 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1251 (the Project Settings that pick a Trade or Location leave out deactivated ones; "Archive" on screen; a follow-up to RP-1239). Setup: a Project of C1's (Contractor, its creator and Project Admin), with K1 as its Consultant; seam 2 adds OW (an Owner), C2 (another Contractor) and OT, the Authorized Person and Admin of another Company's own Project. Seam 1 adds C3, another Company's Project Creator with its own Project. No new read: the writes below are the Project Admin's only, as before, and the lists they feed are read as before.

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1251-1 | Add an Approved Supplier on a deactivated Trade (`POST /v1/projects/:projectId/approved-suppliers`, `app.add_approved_supplier`); a supplier already on one | The Project Admin; the Admin's own Company's other Project Members; every other Company's Authorized Person and Project Members on the Project; another Project's Admin | The Admin is refused (`trade_not_found`, as for a Trade that isn't the Project's) and adds on an active Trade; everyone else gets the 404 that names nothing (db: `not_found`), on an active Trade too. A supplier already on the deactivated Trade stays listed, read by the Project's Members and hidden from another Project's Admin (seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1251-visibility.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1251-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |

Everyone but the Admin gets the 404 that names nothing (seam 1 and seam 2 as above) |
