# RP-1262 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1262: a withdrawn Participant's Vacancies pass to the replacement Participant's Step Pool (ADR 0028; GLOSSARY › Vacancy; workflow-engine.md §9; visibility.md "A Participant's withdrawal"). Their setup: Project "Tower", C1 the Host Company (its Authorized Person is the Project Admin), K1 the Consultant, withdrawn while it holds C1's item, C2 a Contractor, and K2 the Consultant C1 invites in K1's place (at seam 2 also K3 and K4, two more Consultants). Seam 1 `apps/api/test/rp-1262-visibility.test.ts`, seam 2 `packages/db/test/rp-1262-vacancy-rls.test.ts`.

The replacement is the one active Participant of the withdrawn Participant's Project Role whose Visibility covers the item, never of the withdrawn Participant's own Company (decided 2026-11-09 by the product owner). The Vacancy passes when that Participant accepts its Invitation or its Visibility is set.
