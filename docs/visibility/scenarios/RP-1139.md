# RP-1139 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1139 (V14: "No other Company reads a Member's id; names only where V14 and the Record already name them", owner's grill 2026-10-31, docs PR #535), for the Member ids on an item's events and Documents. Setup: C1's Submittal on the Owner's review Workflow. C1's engineer uploads a Document in the Draft, C1 sends and Submits it, K1's manager Recommends A, and the Owner's representative Confirms A, which closes the item. C2, a second Contractor on the Project, covers another Trade and doesn't see the item. C1's AP created the Project, invited K1, the Owner and C2, and sets the numbering pattern. Rows RP-1139-1 to -4 are PR 1 (the events and Documents), RP-1139-5 to -8 PR 2 (the rest of V14's Member ids).

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1139-1 | The app role reads no event actor or Document uploader directly | any Member | `work_item_event.actor_member_id` and `document.uploaded_by_member_id` are not granted to the app role: a direct select is refused. They are read through `app.event_actor_members` and `app.document_uploaders`, which give the id only for a Member of the reader's own Company (seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1139-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
| RP-1139-2 | C1 at the Owner's review, and after it closes, reads no other Company's actor | C1 engineer and PM; K1 engineer and manager; the Owner | On the shared events, `app.event_actor_members` gives C1 its own Members' ids (the PM's Submit) and none of K1's manager (the Recommendation) or the Owner's representative (the closing Confirm); K1 reads its manager's id, the Owner its representative's, and neither reads C1's. The history (`app.work_item_history`) and the closed card (`GET /v1/work-items`, closer) name each reader's own Company's people only, other Companies by name (seam 2: same test; seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1139-visibility.test.ts`) |
| RP-1139-3 | Another Company reads a Document's Company, never its uploader's id | C1 engineer and PM; K1 engineer and manager; the Owner | `app.document_uploaders` gives C1 its engineer's id on C1's Document, which K1 and the Owner read too, and gives them no id. The Documents read (`GET /v1/work-items/:id/documents`, `uploadedBy`) names the uploader to their own Company only, and the Company to everyone who sees the Document (same tests) |
| RP-1139-4 | C2 on C1's item, which it doesn't see, gets nothing | C2 engineer | Asked with the item's event and Document ids, `app.event_actor_members` and `app.document_uploaders` give C2 no row; C1 reads its own. Through the api C2 reads none of the item's Documents or history, which C1 reads; and no read C1, K1 or the Owner gets of the item (Documents, history, the item, the Kanban) carries another Company's Member id (same tests) |
| RP-1139-5 | The app role reads no item creator, numbering setter, inviter, withdrawer or Project creator directly | K1's AP and engineer; C1's engineer | `work_item.created_by_member_id`, `numbering_pattern.set_by_member_id`, `participant.invited_by_member_id` and `withdrawn_by_member_id`, and `project.creator_member_id` are not granted to the app role: a direct select is refused, of rows the reader sees (K1's own Participant row, invited by C1's AP) and to one's own Company too; the rest of each row stays readable. Through the api the Project, its Participants, numbering, Activity Feed and the item answer C1, K1 and the Owner, and none gives K1, the Owner or C2 the id of C1's AP or of C1's raiser (same tests) |
| RP-1139-6 | "Items I'm on" holds the raiser's item without the creator column | C1 engineer (raiser); C1 PM; K1 engineer, who sees the item but never acted; the Owner; C2 engineer | `app.my_created_work_items` gives C1's engineer their raised item and no one else; the Activity Feed's "Items I'm on" (`GET /v1/projects/:id/activity?mine=true`, `onItem`) holds it for the raiser, not for K1's engineer nor C2 (same tests) |
| RP-1139-7 | The numbering pattern's setter is named to the setter's own Company only | C1 AP and engineer; K1 AP, engineer and manager; the Owner; C2 engineer | `app.numbering_pattern_versions` and `GET /v1/projects/:id/numbering` (`savedBy.member`) name C1's AP to C1, and give K1, the Owner and C2 no name (same tests) |
| RP-1139-8 | A withdrawn Participant's withdrawer is no one's id to read | C1 AP (withdraws C2); C2 AP; K1 manager and engineer; the Owner | After C1's AP withdraws C2 (`app.withdraw_participant`, RP-952), `participant.withdrawn_by_member_id` holds C1's AP; a direct select is refused to C2's AP and to C1's AP, who still reads C2's row as withdrawn. Through the api no read K1 or the Owner gets (the Project, its Participants, the Activity Feed) carries the withdrawer's id (same tests) |
