# RP-1139 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1139 (V14: "No other Company reads a Member's id; names only where V14 and the Record already name them", owner's grill 2026-10-31, docs PR #535), for the Member ids on an item's events and Documents. Setup: C1's Submittal on the Owner's review Workflow. C1's engineer uploads a Document in the Draft, C1 sends and Submits it, K1's manager Recommends A, and the Owner's representative Confirms A, which closes the item. C2, a second Contractor on the Project, covers another Trade and doesn't see the item. The rest of V14's Member ids (the item's creator, the numbering pattern's setter, the Participant's inviter and withdrawer, the Project's creator) are RP-1255.

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1139-1 | The app role reads no event actor or Document uploader directly | any Member | `work_item_event.actor_member_id` and `document.uploaded_by_member_id` are not granted to the app role: a direct select is refused. They are read through `app.event_actor_members` and `app.document_uploaders`, which give the id only for a Member of the reader's own Company (seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1139-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
| RP-1139-2 | C1 at the Owner's review, and after it closes, reads no other Company's actor | C1 engineer and PM; K1 engineer and manager; the Owner | On the shared events, `app.event_actor_members` gives C1 its own Members' ids (the PM's Submit) and none of K1's manager (the Recommendation) or the Owner's representative (the closing Confirm); K1 reads its manager's id, the Owner its representative's, and neither reads C1's. The history (`app.work_item_history`) and the closed card (`GET /v1/work-items`, closer) name each reader's own Company's people only, other Companies by name (seam 2: same test; seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1139-visibility.test.ts`) |
| RP-1139-3 | Another Company reads a Document's Company, never its uploader's id | C1 engineer and PM; K1 engineer and manager; the Owner | `app.document_uploaders` gives C1 its engineer's id on C1's Document, which K1 and the Owner read too, and gives them no id. The Documents read (`GET /v1/work-items/:id/documents`, `uploadedBy`) names the uploader to their own Company only, and the Company to everyone who sees the Document (same tests) |
| RP-1139-4 | C2 on C1's item, which it doesn't see, gets nothing | C2 engineer | Asked with the item's event and Document ids, `app.event_actor_members` and `app.document_uploaders` give C2 no row; C1 reads its own. Through the api C2 reads none of the item's Documents or history, which C1 reads; and no read C1, K1 or the Owner gets of the item (Documents, history, the item, the Kanban) carries another Company's Member id (same tests) |
