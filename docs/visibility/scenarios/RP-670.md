# RP-670 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-670, in its setup (Project "Tower", C1, C2, K1, OR, OW).

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-670-1 | A record job fails and then runs out of attempts, and a Rabaed Engineer reads the failing and dead record jobs in Rabaed Admin, retries the dead one and reads the record's Re-produce history | C1's and K1's PMs (who see the item); a Member of a Company not on the Project; a Rabaed Engineer; the app role | A Rabaed Engineer reads each failing and dead record job with its error, attempts and Work Item. No Member reads the error: the item reads "being prepared" while the job still has attempts and no record once it is dead, and no read of C1's or K1's PM (item, history, bell, lists) carries the error text; a Member of a Company not on the Project gets the plain 404 for the item and its record, and no error in the bell. The app role reads no outbox row, and has no grant on `record_job_retry` or the Engineer's functions. The retry is a fresh job: the dead job is kept as it was (attempts, dead time and error), and a Re-produce that was carried out is not retried. Every read and every retry is an `admin_action` with the Engineer's reason (seam 1 `apps/admin/test/record-jobs.test.ts`, seam 2 `packages/db/test/documental-record-checks-rls.test.ts`) |
