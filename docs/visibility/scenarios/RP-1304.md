# RP-1304 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1304: the "Whole Project" Location, for Work Items that apply to the whole Project, such as a general specification (GLOSSARY › Location; data-model.md §3; visibility.md row 4 and V12). Their setup: Project "Tower", C1 the Host Company (its Authorized Person is the Project Admin), K1 the Consultant, Buildings A and B at the top of its Locations, the Trades Electrical and Mechanical, and C1's Electrical item at the Whole Project, Submitted to K1. Seam 1 `apps/api/test/rp-1304-visibility.test.ts`, seam 2 `packages/db/test/rp-1304-visibility-rls.test.ts`.

Any Location covers it (decided 2026-11-14 by the product owner, RP-1304 comment 15861): within the Companies that already see the item, a Member whose Visibility covers any Location of the Project sees a Whole Project item, while its Trade and other dimension values must still be covered. "Whole Project" is not a value a grant lists, and existing grants needed no change.
