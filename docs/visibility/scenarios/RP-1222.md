# RP-1222 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1222 (the New Snag's short form, in RP-1152's create panel over the Snag List), in its setup (a Project of C1's, with C1 as the Contractor and K1 as the Consultant; C1 and K1 each see every Trade and Location).

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
