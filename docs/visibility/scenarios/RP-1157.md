# RP-1157 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1157 (the Activity Feed page to the owner's reference). Setup: a Project of C1's (Contractor), with K1 as its Consultant (an Engineer and another Member who is not on the item), an Owner, and C2 as another Contractor. C1's Engineer raises an item, C1's PM Submits it, and K1's Engineer holds it at K1's review. Every other row, name and label of the feed is read as before (visibility.md "Activity Feed", V5, V14, V19; RP-1129's and RP-1140's tests).

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1157-1 | Each Activity Feed entry says whether the viewer is on its item (`onItem`, `app.activity_feed`'s `on_item`): "You're on this item" on the page | C1's Engineer and PM, K1's Engineer (on it); another Member of C1 or K1, the Owner Representative, the Owner (not on it); C2 | The flag is the viewer's own: set on the item's entries for each Member on it (raised it, holds or held a Step of it, acted on it, or watches it: the rule "Only items I'm on" filters by), and for nobody else, though each of them reads the item's entries. So it never tells a Member that someone else, of their own Company or another, is on the item. C2 reads none of the item's entries (seam 1: `apps/api/test/activity-feed.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1157-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
| RP-1157-2 | Each entry says whether the viewer took it (`byMe`, `by_me`): "(you)" after the viewer's own name | C1's PM (took the Submit); C1's Engineer, K1's Members, the Owner Representative, the Owner | Set on the Submit for C1's PM only. Another Member of C1 reads the PM's name with no flag; K1 and the Owner read the Submit by C1's name only, with no person and no flag (V14) (seam 1: `apps/api/test/activity-feed.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1157-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
