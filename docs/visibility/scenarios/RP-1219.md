# RP-1219 scenarios

Rows of the [scenario matrix](../../visibility.md#scenario-matrix-becomes-the-automated-visibility-test-suite) added under RP-1219 (the Kanban's Draft column; V3, row 73), on one Project with two Contractors, C1 and C2, each raising Submittal Drafts, and K1 as its Consultant. Every side sees all of the Project's Trades and Locations.

| # | Scenario | Who | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP-1219-1 | C1's engineer starts three Submittal Drafts ("Busbar trunking", "Cable tray risers", "Anchor bolts", in that order) and C2's engineer one; each side opens the Submittals Kanban | C1 engineer and PM; C2 engineer; K1 engineer and manager | The Kanban tells C1's and C2's engineers `mayRaise` (they may raise a Submittals Type, `app.raisable_type_ids`, the rule of the Register Import's 403) and gives them the Draft column first, holding only their own Participant's Drafts: C1's three to C1, by Subject (Anchor bolts, Busbar trunking, Cable tray risers), never in the order they were started; C2's one to C2. Neither reads the other Contractor's Drafts, in the column or anywhere on the board. K1's Members may raise no Submittal: `mayRaise` is false and the board has no Draft Stage, no Draft column (not even an empty one) and no Draft in its Stage field, and K1 reads none of the Drafts (seam 1: `apps/api/test/rp-1219-visibility.test.ts`; seam 2: `packages/db/test/rp-1219-visibility-rls.test.ts`) |
