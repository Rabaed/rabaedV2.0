# Visibility specification

**The #1 rule of Rabaed: no one sees another company's work unless a rule below explicitly allows it.** When a case isn't covered, the answer is *hidden* until a rule is added here.

Terms follow [GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Enforcement follows [ADR 0007](adr/0007-project-is-the-tenancy-boundary.md) (Project is the tenancy boundary, row-level security) and [data-model.md](data-model.md).

## The layers

A Member sees a thing only if it passes **every** layer, in order.

| # | Layer | Question | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instance | Is it on my Instance? | Separate deployments |
| 2 | Project | Is my Company a Participant, and am I an active Project Member? | RLS on `project_id` |
| 3 | Participant access | Has my Participant been given access to this Work Item? (it raised it, it handles a Step, oversight, or a Submit informed it) | `work_item_access` + RLS |
| 4 | Member Visibility | Does my own Visibility cover the item's Trade, Location and other dimension values? While a Sent Back item is held by its raiser, everyone else's values are those it had at the Send Back (V19; as built, RP-582: `work_item.built_ins_as_arrived`, read by `app.sees_work_item`). | RLS join on `visibility_grant` |
| 5 | Audience | Is this history entry shared, or internal to my Participant? | `work_item_event.audience` |

**Exceptions** show a sealed Documental Record PDF or an item's Document Number and Subject, never the live item:
- **E1 Link:** you see the Document Number and Subject of an item linked from one you can see, and can open its Documental Record (from Form engine part 4).
- **E2 Signatory Access:** you keep, forever, the Documental Records of items you signed, and your Company keeps those its Members signed.
- **E3 Linked from:** you see the Document Number and Subject of an item that has been Submitted (it has a Submission Date) and links to one you can see. Nothing more, ever: not its Documental Record (settled 2026-10-05).

## Rules

- **V1.** Draft and internal-Stage Work Items are visible only inside the raising Participant, until the item is first Submitted. An item Sent Back to its raiser after it was Submitted stays visible to every Participant with access to it, as it was at the Send Back (V19, ADR 0014; as built, RP-309: `app.sees_work_item` keeps an item with a Submission Date visible at its raiser's own Steps).
  - **Drafts visible to** (settled 2026-10-10, ADR 0019; as built, RP-514): a setting of the Draft Step. "The author's whole Company" (`company`, the default, as before): every Member of the raising Participant who sees the item reads the Draft, and only its holder edits it (form-engine.md §4). "The author only" (`author`): a Draft (an item that never left its Draft Step, so has no Document Number) is seen only by the Member holding it; every other Member, the PM and the Authorized Person included, gets the 404 of any hidden item, and it is in no List, Kanban or count of theirs (`app.sees_work_item`, so every channel reading the item follows). The Authorized Person still hands it over: the Handover lists it by its Step and Project only, as any item they don't see (RP-108-2), and the new holder then sees it. Other Companies never see a Draft under either setting. Once it leaves the Draft Step, the raising Participant reads it as before. Scenario RP-514-1.
- **V2.** A Participant gains access to a Work Item only when:
  - it raised the item, or
  - a Workflow Step is assigned to it, or
  - it is an Owner or Owner Representative, the item has been Submitted, and its Visibility covers the item (*oversight*), or
  - a Submit naming its role to inform was taken on the item, and its Visibility covered the item then (*informed*, RP-625).

  Walking skeleton (RP-194): oversight is granted when the item is Submitted, from the Owner's or Owner Representative's Visibility at that moment. Narrowing it later hides the item at once (layer 4); widening it doesn't yet reach items already Submitted.

  **Informed** (as built, RP-625; workflow-engine.md §5.1): a Submit may name roles to inform (the Snag's Raise informs the Consultant). Taking it gives every active Participant of those roles whose Visibility covers all the item's dimension values access with reason `informed` (`app.inform_participants`), never a Participant of the raiser's role or of the role the Submit hands it to (V3), and nothing more to one that already has access. An informed Participant reads the item as any Participant without the Step does: its shared moves only (V5: the holder's moves inside it, its Internal Notes and pick-ups stay its own), its Stage and Step Age from the last shared move (V14; a Submit or Send Back that stays inside the holder, as a Contractor's own Raise and Resolve do, is one: `app.submit_reaches_others`; a Send or Return, such as a Contractor's internal review, never is, RP-625-5), and its answers, Documents and Links as they arrived there (V19). It never holds a Step, is offered no Transition, gets no "Step reached", and Need My Action never counts it. As with oversight, narrowing its Members' Visibility hides the item at once (layer 4), and widening a Participant's Visibility doesn't reach items already Submitted. Scenarios RP-625-1 to RP-625-5.
- **V3.** Two Participants in the same Project Role never see each other's Work Items, even when they share a Trade.
- **V4.** A Member never sees more than their Participant. A Member's Visibility is a subset of their Participant's.
- **V5.** Internal Communication (Internal Notes, internal Step moves, Returns, internal approvals, Recommended Codes) is visible only to Members of the Participant where it happened. A Recommended Code to the Owner (ADR 0022), and the moves of the Owner's review that carry or send it back, are read by the Consultant and the Participant holding the review only (scenarios RP-593-1 to RP-593-3). An Internal Note stays internal even when it is written with a Transition that crosses to another Participant, such as Submit; anything meant for the other side goes in Chat or the Form. A reply's answers (the Action Form of the Transition a Participant takes, built from a Screen, ADR 0019) follow the Transition event's audience, and a field the Screen marks internal stays internal even on a shared Transition such as a Code: the Consultant's verification note or Recommended Code never reaches the Contractor, while its Remarks do (scenario RP-516-1). As built (RP-516): `app.take_transition` writes the answers to the pinned Screen Version's internal fields in an `internal_answers` event internal to the acting Participant, just before the Transition's own, whose payload keeps the shared ones; both reach the history (`app.work_item_history`'s `answers`) through the same row-level security. As built (RP-1079; scenarios RP-1079-1, RP-1079-2): a reply Screen's files follow the event that took them too (`document.reply_event_id`), whatever Transition took them, so the files an internal move takes stay with its Participant, and the photos and files a Recommendation to the Owner takes are read by the Consultant and the Participant holding the review only, during the review, after an Owner Send Back and after the Confirm, whoever holds the item then. The documents policy and `app.document_times` (the api's list and downloads), a Validate rule's "has a Document" and the annotated copy's start require that event, or either copy of it the Owner's review writes, to be one the Member reads (`app.reply_event_seen`); what shows every reader one content, `app.work_item_shared_documents`, the Working Copy and the Documental Record, requires it to be shared (`app.reply_event_shared`).
- **V6.** Chat is visible to every Participant that currently has access to the Work Item.
- **V7.** A closed item's Documental Record names everyone who took a Transition on its path to the outcome, whichever Company they belong to: for each, the Member's name, Position, Company, the date, their Signature and what they wrote in the Transition's shared Action Form, with each acting Company's logo and Stamp (ADR 0017, ADR 0023). Returns and the work around them, Internal Notes, Recommended Codes (an internal reviewer's own Code included: his card reads "Reviewed", RP-615 to decide otherwise; a Code the Owner sent back, ADR 0022), Chat and in-progress answers stay out, and the first entry is dated at the raiser's first Transition on the path to the outcome, never the Draft's start: a send out of the Draft that was Returned is part of the work kept off the record, so "Prepared" carries the last one (settled 2026-10-18, Main 2). Only the sealed record names another Company's people: the live item's screens (history, With, Kanban, notifications) and the Working Copy keep V14. Who may download it (settled 2026-10-16, RP-23 grilling): everyone who can see the closed item (layers 1–4, oversight included), plus E1 and E2.
- **V8.** Removal from a Project, a Participant's withdrawal, or Project closure never takes away E2.
- **A Participant's withdrawal** (settled 2026-10-31, ADR 0028, RP-1074; not built yet). Only the Host Company's Project Admin withdraws one; its confirmation shows counts only (items to be Cancelled, Steps to become Vacancies). The withdrawn Company's Authorized Person and Project Members are told, by email and in-app, naming the Host Company and not the person. **Other Companies get no withdrawal notice**, since that would reveal who is on the Project: they learn only through their own items, by the notices those already send ("Cancelled by Rabaed: ‹Company› left the Project" on an item it raised, which they already know). On an item whose Step it held, now a Vacancy, every other Company reads **"Waiting for a new ‹role›"** (its role, never a Company), the item leaves the "With" filter's old Company, and Step Age keeps counting until the replacement Participant receives it. Everything the withdrawn Company did stays as it was (history, replies, files, signatures); its Members keep only E2.
- **V9.** A Rabaed Engineer sees data only through Rabaed Admin, and every access is logged with a reason.

## Leak channels

Every one of these must apply the same layers. A new feature that adds a channel must add it here (every notification and outbox kind is checked by `scripts/check-leak-channels.test.ts`, RP-375).

| Channel | Rule |
|---|---|

A card's Contractor name is the raiser's Company, which Search already finds. The Kanban's lanes inside one's own Company are its roles (the holder's Position, or a Step not picked up, its Pool's first Position, read through `project_member_position`, which RLS gives one's own Participant only); another Company is one lane by its name, never its roles (V5). As built (RP-434): `app.transition_follow_up_items` gives each Comment the reviewed item's access rows, Trade, Location and Scopes, and routes it to the item's raiser, never by role; a Send Back returns to the Participant that held the Step (`app.next_step_holder`). Scenarios RP-434-1 to RP-434-3 The Snags a code raises from failed check items (RP-577) are raised the same way, with the same access: seen exactly where the Inspection is, held by its raiser; their copied photos read by whoever sees the Snag, and the check item they name only through `app.raised_from_check_item` for whoever sees the Snag (scenario RP-577-1). | Dashboard and Location Status | Aggregates are computed over the viewer's visible items only. No project-wide totals for a Contractor. Dashboard cards count Revision chains by the latest Revision the viewer can see; **Pending** counts open items that have been Submitted, and the **In preparation** figure (Drafts and internal review) is shown only to the raiser's Participant (V1). The Revision line's figures (Code C, or a Failed inspection, RP-437) follow the same rule: another Company sees "awaiting revision" while the raiser's Revision is still in Draft or internal review. Every number opens the List with the same filter, so a count never disagrees with the items behind it (settled 2026-10-06). |
| Search and filters | Search results are filtered by the same RLS. No "12 results, 3 shown". Search covers Document Number, Subject, Type, Trade, Location and the raiser's Company name only, never Form answers or Documents, so neither the raiser's changes after a Send Back (V19) nor a reply's answers can be found (settled 2026-10-06). As built (RP-347): the List's and the Kanban's `q`, through `app.search_work_items` over an index the app role can't read; a search's Stage counts are of the List's page, or the Kanban's shown cards, only. As built (RP-410): the same `q` also finds, as one phrase, what the card shows besides: its owner as the viewer reads it (their own Company's person; another Company by name only, never its people, V14) and the Zone, Building or Floor its Location is in (scenario RP-410-1). As built (RP-639): `q` also finds the Paper ref, which everyone who sees the item reads and which never changes once Submitted. |
| Direct URL or ID | An inaccessible item returns **404, never 403**, so its existence isn't revealed. IDs are UUIDs and can't be guessed. As built (RP-520): the web app shows every 404 (a missing page, a hidden Project, a hidden item) on one not-found page that reads nothing about the request: "We can't find this page. It may not exist, or you may not have access to it. Ask the person who sent you the link." and a Projects button, never which of the two it is (scenario RP-520-1). |
| Files Module | Work Item folders appear only for visible items. Free folders follow folder permissions. |
| File downloads | Short-lived signed URLs, issued only after an access check. A Document follows its Work Item: who can't see the item gets 404 for its Documents and their URLs. Files are stored under their Project's prefix, and only the api signs (ADR 0007). A Document added while the item is held by another Participant (its raiser, after a Send Back) is that Participant's until the item leaves it: everyone else gets 404 for it, like one that doesn't exist (V19, RP-309), after the close as well (ADR 0027; scenario RP-955-1). A reply Screen's file (ADR 0024, RP-623) is the holding Participant's in the same way until the Transition that takes it, then shared with everyone who sees the item; one no Transition took is removed when the item leaves that Participant (scenarios RP-623-1 to RP-623-3). So is a reply Screen's checklist (RP-86): its answers (`reply_checklist`, read by the holding Participant only) and its items' photos are that Participant's until the code takes them into its shared event (scenario RP-86-1). A frozen original takes no new copy. Scenario RP-656-1. A reply Screen's photo (RP-981) is annotated by whoever may add to its field now, and its copy is a reply Document of the same field and check item, so the holding Participant's until the Transition takes both, and removed with the original when the item leaves it any other way; a copy is never written where its original isn't (scenario RP-981-1). |
| Form `member` and `participant` fields | Offer only the Project Members and Participants the filler can see (V15): their own Participant's Members; their own Participant, the Host Company's and the Companies on the item. A saved id they couldn't have been offered is refused like a made-up one. Another Company reads a `member` answer as the Company's name only, without the Member's id (V14). |
| Pins on shared Drawings | Show only Pins of visible items. A shared floor plan must not show a competitor's Snags. |
| Links | Show a target's number, title and Documental Record (E1). Never its live data or history. A target the viewer can't see reaches the browser as its number and title only, without its id, and opening it says only that the viewer may not see its details, on the Link's own page by the Link's id (RP-521); the item's own URL still returns 404. |
| Linked from | Lists the items that link to one the viewer can see, with number and title only for those the viewer can't see (E3). Only items that have been Submitted at least once are listed, never one that hasn't left its raiser yet (Draft or internal review). An item Sent Back to its raiser stays listed, with its Links as they were at the Send Back, until it is Submitted again (settled 2026-10-06, RP-295). |
| Form Sections filled by another Participant | Retired (ADR 0019; RP-437): the Form is the raiser's, and every other Participant answers in its reply Screen ("Screens and replies"). Publishing a Form refuses a section whose `editable_at` names another Participant's Step (`other_participant_section`), and nobody but the raiser's Member holding the Step saves the Form (`app.can_save_answers`). An item still pinned to MAR Form Version 4 keeps its Consultant verification read-only, as it arrived, to everyone. |
| An item Sent Back to its raiser | Everyone with access goes on seeing it, as it was at the Send Back, until the raiser Submits it again (V19): its answers (checklist items and link questions included), its Documents (the Attachments System Field, file-field uploads, photos and checklist photos), its Links (free and `relies_on`) and its Trade, Location and Scopes (at a Step that lets the raiser change them, RP-580), in every read: who sees it at all (layer 4), its List and Kanban rows, their Trade and Location filters and sorts, search, the Home and Dashboard counts, the item, its Documents and their downloads, its Links, Linked from, Link search, the history and Activity Feed, notifications, and the tables themselves through the app role. As built (RP-309): `app.item_row_seen`, applied by the RLS on `document` and `work_item_link`, `app.work_item_links` and `app.work_item_linked_from`; the answers through `data_as_arrived` (RP-304). As built (RP-582): Trade, Location and Scopes through `work_item.built_ins_as_arrived`, taken before the raiser's first change and cleared when the item leaves; `app.sees_work_item` (layer 4), the views `work_item_dimension_value_seen` and `work_item_scope_seen` (which the api's List, Kanban, Home, Dashboard, filters, sorts and item read use), the policies on `work_item_dimension_value` and `work_item_scope` (the rows as they are now: the holder's Participant only) and the search index (`app.refresh_work_item_search`, the snapshot's names) apply it. Changes write no shared event and notify nobody outside the raiser. |
| Creation Date | Seen only by the raiser's Participant; other Companies see the Submission Date, so they never learn how long the raiser worked on it. When a Draft was first started is kept for audit, even for a cancelled or discarded Draft, and once the item has its Document Number it is shown to nobody, at every layer (database role, api, screen); while it is still a Draft, the Members working on it see each other's real save times (settled 2026-10-06). Its channels (RP-373 grilling, 2026-10-06): **ids** are random, so an id carries no time (ADR 0015), and un-numbered Drafts in the List and Kanban come last, ordered by Subject, then id; **answer times and "Saved"**: read by every Member of the raiser's Participant (not only the one who may save, RP-525) and by no other Company (`app.work_item_field_times`; another Participant saving its own Form Section reads its own Company's times only); once numbered, a time earlier than the Creation Date reads as the Creation Date, from then on, after a Send Back too, and each Revision by its own Creation Date (the stored times stay for audit); **Step Age and the weekly report**: an un-numbered Draft has no Step Age and is left out of the report (settled 2026-10-10, ADR 0020, settling RP-364: a Draft shows no Step Age to anyone, its author included, and since a Return stays in Internal Review no Draft is ever numbered; a Returned item's Step Age counts from the Return. | Link search | Offers only items in the same Project that the linker can see and that have been Submitted at least once, including one Sent Back to its raiser (settled 2026-10-06, RP-295). A saved id they couldn't have been offered is refused like a made-up one. |
| Notifications and emails | Sent only to Members with access, checked again at send time. The subject line holds only the Document Number, Subject and what happened. A notification's text follows V14: another Company by name only. Per-Transition rules a Workflow adds later never override a Member's own notification settings (settled 2026-10-06). In-app is always sent (decided 2026-10-08): a Member has no In-app switch, only an email choice per group, Pause all email and a mute per Project. A Transition may name extra recipients: the raiser, watchers, a Position of the acting Participant, never a person, a group or an email address; they are delivered as watched-item notifications, so only to Members who see the item at send time, with the same V5 rule, and through the Member's own settings (RP-432: `app.transition_notice_members`, then `app.notify_member`; an engineer of another Company, or a Member narrowed off the item after the move, hears nothing). As built (RP-355): `app.deliver_notification` re-checks `app.sees_work_item` for each recipient at delivery, and a watched item's event reaches a watcher only if they may read it (V5: an internal move stays inside its Participant); a notification's text names another Company only by name. A Sent Back notification goes only to the Members of the Participant it was sent back to who see the item, and names the Company that sent it back by name only; (RP-356's Vacancy notification went with the member-level Vacancy, RP-108: a Member's Steps are handed over before they leave.) Emails (RP-357): `app.take_notification_email` checks again at send time, as the recipient (`app.sees_work_item`, V5), and sends nothing once the notification is withdrawn, email paused, the Project muted or closed; the subject is the Document Number, Subject and what happened, and the body names another Company by name, with the final Code's signer only. As built (RP-155): "record ready" (kind `record_ready`, outbox kind `record_ready`, queued when a record is published) goes to each active Member of the Project who sees the closed item at delivery (`app.deliver_record_ready`, `app.sees_work_item` as that Member), unless they muted the Project; under the Member's "Items I watch" setting (the bell, plus email immediately or in the daily digest, RP-950), and it names no Company and no person, only the item the Member already reads, linking to the item and never to the file. A closed Project goes quiet (RP-360, scenario 15): `app.deliver_notification` delivers nothing of a Project that is no longer active, so events still in the outbox when it closed reach nobody, in the bell or by email, and every email (immediate, the daily digest's entries) checks it again at send time; notifications already delivered stay readable. A Package Submit (RP-643, scenario RP-588-2) sends one `package_submitted` notification per Member it reaches instead of a "Step reached" per submittal: delivered only for the items that Member sees (`app.notify_member_of_package`), routed as "Step reached", and counting the items whose Step reached that Member and that they still see, naming the Package and showing only while that is at least one, at every read and at send time. |
| Daily email digest | One email a morning (07:00 Riyadh, Sunday to Thursday) per Member, of their own notifications routed to it, each entry checked again at send time exactly as an immediate email is, as the recipient (`app.notification_email_content`: `app.sees_work_item`, V5, withdrawn, email paused, the Project muted or closed, the setting changed). Its subject names nothing; each entry names another Company by name only, with the final Code's signer only (V14). As built (RP-358): `app.enqueue_notification_digests` queues one per Member with entries waiting on an active Project, and `app.take_notification_digest` takes them. |
| Password reset emails | A forgotten password's link (RP-1058; outbox kind `password_reset`). It names no Work Item, Project, Company or person: only a link to set a new password, in the Member's language. Asking for one gives any email the same answer in no less time, and the same refusal past the limits (per email and per visitor, counting every request alike), so it never tells whether an email belongs to a Member; only an active Member of an active Company is emailed. The token is minted as the email is sent and stored only as a hash, in no table the app role or `rabaed_admin` reads; the link carries it in the URL fragment, and no log holds it, the email or the password. Using it signs the Member out everywhere else. |
| Participant Invitation emails | A Project Admin's invitation to a Company on Rabaed (RP-1072; ADR 0009; outbox kind `participant_invitation`). It goes to the invited Company's active Authorized Person only, in their language, and names only what they see of the invitation before accepting: the Project's name, the Host Company and the offered Project Role (V15), never another Participant; it links to their Company's Projects, where they accept or decline. | Need My Action | Counts only open assignments the viewer holds and not-picked-up Steps in their Step Pool, all of them items they can see. A pool item a colleague picked up leaves it. |
| Watch | A Member watches only items they can see; a watcher who loses sight of the item gets nothing more. Nobody sees who else watches an item, nor how many do. A watched chain's later Revision reaches another Company's watcher only once it is Submitted (V1) (settled 2026-10-06). |
| Working Copy | The PDF of an item that isn't closed (settled 2026-10-16, RP-23 grilling; ADR 0023), made on request for anyone who sees the item, from the same read as Download (`GET /v1/work-items/:id/shared`, Exports and handover row): the same for every viewer, Companies by name only, no person, no Signature, no Stamp, no Verification Code, unsealed, watermarked with the Project's text. A Draft's Working Copy only for the Members who see the Draft (V1). ## Settled rules (2026-09-26)

- **V10. Approved Supplier List.** Entries are Project-wide: every Participant sees the supplier name and its approval, never the submittal that approved it. As built (RP-592): `approved_supplier`, read by every Project Member of every Participant through row-level security on `project_id`, with column grants to the app role for its name, Trade, Scope and status only: never `source`, `source_work_item_id`, nor who added or withdrew it and when (another Company's people, V14, and when a SAR closed). Only the Project's Project Admins add or withdraw an entry (`app.add_approved_supplier`, `app.withdraw_approved_supplier`); anyone else, another Project's Project Admin included, gets the plain 404. A withdrawn entry stays listed, withdrawn (scenario RP-592-1). A Supplier Approval Request closed at Code A or B adds its supplier itself (RP-594, `app.sar_adds_supplier`): another Contractor of the Project reads the supplier, never the SAR, and has no grant on `source_work_item_id` (scenario RP-594-1).
- **V11. Drawing register.** Approved, current Drawing Revisions are visible to every Participant whose Visibility covers the Drawing's Trade. While under review, only the Participants with access to the review Work Item can see them.
- **V13. Forms are all-or-nothing.** Anyone who can see a Work Item sees its entire Form and Documents. There are no private fields; only Internal Communication is hidden (V5), and answers still being filled in at another Participant's Step (V19). Another Participant's reply is not part of the Form: it follows its Transition event's audience, its Screen's internal fields internal to it (V5, ADR 0019; RP-516).
- **V14. Workflow view.** In an item's "View workflow" diagram, the Workflow's map is shown whole, every Company's Steps included (V20, ADR 0016), but what happened on the item at another company's internal Steps is collapsed into one block ("<Company> review"): no names, no internal Returns, no Recommended Code, and not which of its internal Steps holds the item. Only the hand-overs (Submit) and the final outcome with its signer are shown. (The closed item's Documental Record names everyone who acted, V7.) **No other Company reads a Member's id** (settled 2026-10-31, RP-1139; not built yet): where a Member of another Company may be named (the final signer, the Record), the reader gets the name only, never the id, on any event, answer or read, so a stable id can't follow one person across items. As found by RP-1129: `work_item_event.actor_member_id` is granted to the app role today; the api never shows it.
  The same grouping applies to **every screen that shows Steps or holders**: Kanban swimlanes, the list's **"With"** column (never "Current owner"), report and item steppers, notifications. The viewer's own company appears in full; another company appears as one grouped lane or cell with the company name and never a person's name. The build uses one shared component for this. As built (RP-519): a Kanban column header names the Steps of its Stage that the viewer's own Participant holds items at, each with its count (`columns[].steps` of the board, from the work item query), and narrows the column to one (its header then counts that Step's items, which are the viewer's own; a Step that leaves the column is cleared, RP-528 review); another Participant's Steps are never in the data.
  **Who holds it, or who it waits on (settled 2026-10-10, ADR 0018).** The holding Participant's own Members read the holder's name ("Ali Sonour · TMC Constructions"), or while it is pooled the pool's names, up to three then "+n" ("TMC Constructions · not picked up yet: Ali Sonour, Khalid Bakr"). Every other Participant reads the holding Company's name only, whether one Member holds it, a pool of one was given it on arrival, or it waits in a pool; the internal events that say so ("Assigned to …, the only one who can take this Step", Pick up, Return to pool) are the holding Participant's own (V5). As built (RP-513): `app.work_item_pool` and the item read's `heldBy.pool`, `app.work_item_holder` for the holder; scenario RP-513-1.
  **With Owner review (ADR 0022; as built in the spec RP-598 review).** While an item waits at the Owner's review (a `confirm_code` Step), the Participant holding the review and the Consultant whose Recommendation brought it there read the holder as above; everyone else (the Contractor, and the Owner while an Owner Representative holds the review) reads "With Owner review" («مع مراجعة المالك»), never the holding Company's name, everywhere the holder shows: the List's "With" (and its Owner sort), the Kanban's card and one lane of that name, keyed by no Participant, the item's "With", its Workflow map and the weekly Step Age report. No holder reaches them, so the "With" and Owner filters, the Kanban's search on the card's owner and the "With" filter's Companies never name it either. As built: `app.with_owner_review` gives the label, read by the work item query, the item read, the map and `app.take_step_age_report`; and the database gives them no holder itself (RP-1138): `app.work_item_holder` and the List row of `app.work_items_seen` name no holding Participant, Member or Company, and `app.work_item_companies` names a review Step's assignment only to its holder and to the Consultant whose transition brought the item there, also after the item has left the review (a Send Back is internal to the two); scenarios RP-598-1 and RP-1138-1.
  **Step Age follows the grouping (settled 2026-09-30, RP-239).** Inside the Company holding the item, Step Age counts from the current internal Step. Every other Company sees it counted from when the item reached the holding Company, so internal moves there never reset or reveal anything. The periodic ageing report works the same way.
- **V12. Access follows current Visibility.** A Member who gains a Trade or Location sees that dimension's items, including earlier ones. A Member who loses it stops seeing them, except under E2.
- **V15. Company Projects and Participants (settled 2026-09-29, RP-214).**
  - Every Member of a Participant, including its Authorized Person before they are a Project Member, sees for each Project their Company takes part in: its name, code and Project Number, the **Host Company**'s name, their own Participant's Project Role, and their own Company's Project Members with their Positions. This is the **Company Projects** view.
  - The Authorized Person manages their Company's Project Members there, even before being a Project Member.
  - A Participant never sees the list of other Participants: not other Contractors, nor the Consultants, Owners or Owner Representatives. Another Company's name appears only as the Host Company, and on Work Items the viewer can access, where that Company raised the item, the item is or was "With" it, or it acted in the part of the item's history the viewer sees (V14). Never its Project Members.
  - Project Admins see every Participant, because they add and manage them.
  - A Company invited to a Project sees only the invitation (Project name, Host Company, offered Project Role) until its Authorized Person accepts it; declined or pending invitations are never shown to other Participants ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-participant-invitation-with-consent.md)).
  - **As built (RP-1051; scenario RP-1051-1):** Company Projects (`GET /v1/participants`, the `/participants` page and its sidebar entry) is every Member's, read through `app.company_participants`, which lists the same Participants as `app.current_participant_ids`: the Authorized Person every one of their Company's, any other Member their Company's on the Projects they are a Project Member of. A Member on none reads an empty list, never a 403. Only the Authorized Person reads the Company's invitations and adds, removes or narrows Project Members; everyone else reads them.
- **V16. Visibility grants (settled 2026-09-29, RP-220; introduced in RP-191).** A Participant's Visibility grant is seen by its own Company and by the Project's Project Admins, who set it. A Project Member's grant is seen only by their own Company, and set only by its Authorized Person, within the Participant's (V4). The Authorized Person also sees the Trades and Locations their Participant covers, with the names of the Locations above them so each one reads in place (Tower 1 › Building A), so they can narrow it for their Members before they are a Project Member themselves (an addition to V15). Likewise they see the Scopes and Sub-scopes under the Trades their Participant covers (RP-263). Nothing else of the Project's Trades, Scopes and Locations reaches them until they are a Project Member. Every Project Member sees the Project's Trades, Scopes and Locations. Only its Project Admins change Scopes; anyone else who tries gets a 404 that names nothing.
  - **Visibility Gap warnings follow the same line.** Project Admins are warned only about values no active Participant covers, worked out from Participant grants alone, so a warning never shows how another Company splits its coverage among its Members. Each Authorized Person is warned, for their own Participant only, about values it covers that none of its Project Members do.
- **V17. Inviting a Member whose email is taken (settled 2026-09-30, RP-234).** An email belongs to one Member on the Instance. When an Authorized Person invites an email that belongs to a Member of another Company, the invitation is refused with a clear answer: the person is already registered on Rabaed with another Company. It never says which Company, and nothing else about that Member. This is a deliberate, narrow exception: the inviter learns only that the person is on Rabaed. An email of their own Company's Member gets the ordinary "already a Member" answer. A deactivated Member of their own Company is reactivated rather than invited again, keeping their Signature and records.
- **V18. Library contents (settled 2026-10-03, RP-18).** A Company's Library (Forms, Saved Fields, Option Lists, Trade and Scope lists) is private to that Company. Something in it becomes visible beyond the Company only when it is used on a Project: every Member of that Project's Participants can then see it (a Form, and the Saved Fields and Option Lists that Form uses) and copy it into their own Company's Library. Nobody can browse another Company's Library. A Project Admin may take a Form from any Participant's Library, but only once that Participant's Authorized Person has agreed to offer it to the Project. Rabaed Defaults are visible to every Company. **Company defaults** (settled 2026-10-10, ADR 0019; as built, RP-517): which Library Form, Workflow and Screen a Company starts a new Project from per Work Item Type (`company_default`) are part of its Library: read by its own Members only, marked and cleared by its Authorized Person only; a Library Form (`form_definition.owner_kind` `company`) is read by its Company only. Creating a Project copies them into it, so what reaches the Project is the Project's copy, read as any of its definitions (V20); a Company that joins gets nothing copied (scenario RP-517-1). **Library Workflows** (as built, RP-442): the Library page lists the Company's own only; saving a Workflow to the Library, which only the Authorized Person does, and using one on a Project, which only that Project's Project Admin does, are copies that keep no link; another Company reads none of it, by list, id, map or builder, until a copy is in a Project it is on (scenario RP-442-1).
- **V20. Workflow definitions (settled 2026-10-08, ADR 0016; built in RP-426).** A Workflow is a description of a process, not anyone's work. The Workflows on a Project (its own copies and the Rabaed Defaults it runs) are readable by every Member of every Participant on it, with every Company's Steps, internal Steps and Returns included, and so is which Workflow each Work Item Type runs there. What happens on an item at another Participant's Steps stays as V5, V14 and V19 say: who holds it, Returns taken, Internal Notes, Recommended Codes and in-progress answers. A Workflow in a Company's Library is private to that Company until it is copied into a Project (V18). A binding that applies only to one raising Participant ("Contractor X's DARs") is read by that Participant's Members and the Project Admins only, since a Participant never learns of another (V15). The Workflow such an exception binds is itself a definition on the Project, readable by every Member there: so its name must never name the Participant (not "Contractor X's DARs" but "DARs, short route"), or the Workflow list tells everyone who the exception is for; WF-4 enforces it when the Workflow is published, renamed or bound (RP-427). A draft Version is shown to nobody through the tables (row-level security on `workflow_version` lets the app role read published Versions only); its authors alone, the Project's Project Admins or for a Library Workflow its Company's Authorized Person, read it, through `app.workflow_draft` (RP-427). Rabaed Defaults are visible to every Company. As built (RP-426): the item read names its Workflow and Version through `app.work_item_workflow`, for whoever sees the item, whether or not they read the Workflow's rows. **Screens are part of the map** (ADR 0019): a Project's Screens are read like its Workflows, by every Member of every Participant on it, once published; a Library's by its own Company only (V18); the Rabaed Default Screens by every Company; a draft Version by its authors only. What a Member wrote in one on an item follows its Transition event's audience and the Screen's internal fields (V5). As built (RP-516): row-level security on `screen` (by `owner_kind`; a Screen with no published Version only for its authors, `app.can_author_screen`) and `screen_version` (published only); a draft through `app.screen_draft`, for its authors only; the app role writes neither table, only through `app.create_screen`, `app.save_screen_draft` and `app.publish_screen`, which answer anyone but the Project's Project Admins `not_found`. As built (RP-441, `GET /v1/projects/:id/workflow-settings`, `GET /v1/workflows/:id/versions/:n`, `GET /v1/projects/:id/screen-pins`): the settings page reads through row-level security as the Member, so a Member gets only the bindings and exceptions the policy gives them (an exception: its Participant and the Project Admins); the Library list and the Project's unpublished copies go to Project Admins only, the Library list being their own Company's; a Workflow Version is read only when published (scenario RP-441-1). **Checklist Templates are part of the map too** (RP-651): a Project's templates and every Version of them are read by every Member of every Participant on it, and written by its Project Admins only; the copy a Work Item makes of one is read with the item, by whoever sees it (scenarios RP-651-1, RP-651-2).
- **V19. The raiser's changes while an item it Submitted is Sent Back to it (settled 2026-10-05, Form engine part 3, ADR 0013; narrowed by ADR 0019, RP-437).** Form Sections filled in by another Participant, and the hiding of their in-progress answers, are retired: the Form belongs to the raiser and every other Participant answers in its reply Screen (V5, "Screens and replies"). As built (RP-437, `20270118100000_retire_other_participant_sections.sql`): publishing a Form refuses a section whose `editable_at` names another Participant's Step (`other_participant_section`); `app.can_save_answers` lets only the raiser's Member holding the Step save; a Send Back out of another Participant's Step puts nothing back, there being nothing it wrote; the "Filled in by" mark is gone. MAR Form Version 4, the last with such a section, stays published for the items pinned to it, its section read-only to everyone. What stays is the raiser's rule after a Send Back: when an item it Submitted is Sent Back to it (settled 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06, RP-295, RP-309): everyone who saw the item goes on seeing it as it was at the Send Back, its answers, Documents (file-field uploads, photos and checklist photos included), checklist items, Links (free and `relies_on`), and Trade, Location and Scopes, so who may see it at all stays as it was too (as built, RP-582, scenarios RP-582-1 and RP-582-2), until the raiser Submits it again; the raiser's changes reach only the raiser's Members until then. The raiser has changes only where the Step the Send Back led to says **After a Send Back: Edit the Form** (ADR 0021; as built, RP-580: `app.can_save_answers` through `app.after_send_back_setting`, scenario RP-580-1); at "Comment only", the default of every Workflow Version saved or published since, it changes nothing until the next Submit, and only Versions published before keep editing (null). The channels: the item and its list rows, its answers, its Documents and their downloads, its Links, Linked from and Link search (where it stays, scenarios 58 and 59), the history and the Activity Feed (its changes are `answers_changed` events internal to the raiser, and Documents write none), notifications (a move inside the raiser reaches only the raiser's Members), and the app role's own reads of the `document` and `work_item_link` tables. The Transition event's content hash covers the item's exact content, Documents included, as every Transition's does, and is read by nobody through the app role (the Documental Record row). **A close shares only what was handed over** (settled 2026-10-31, ADR 0027, RP-955): after a close, by a Code, a Cancel or Rabaed's own Cancel, other Participants see the item as it was at its last arrival with them. A Document or Link the holder added after that stays the holder's, after the close as well, as the Documental Record already prints it (RP-147). As built (RP-955, `20271122000000_close_shares_handed_over.sql`; scenarios in `docs/visibility/scenarios/RP-955.md`): `app.item_row_as_arrived` gives a closed item's rows to everyone who sees it, except those added during the arrival the close follows (`arrival = arrivals - 1`, which the Record leaves out too; a row Rabaed adds after the close, such as a Revision's chain Link, is everyone's), which go only to the Participant that held it at the close (`app.held_at_close`: a Step of its done at the moment the item closed; or one still open). So the document and link policies, downloads, `app.document_times`, `app.work_item_links`, Linked from and `app.raised_from_check_item_photos` all follow it; `app.work_item_shared_documents` and `app.work_item_shared_links` (the Download) read the same rule. A reply file a shared event took is handed over with it, whatever its arrival, so the closing Code's own files (ADR 0024) are everyone's who sees the item (`app.document_seen`, the one rule of the documents policy, `app.document_times` and `app.raised_from_check_item_photos`, with `app.reply_event_seen` in it), as the Record prints them. A Member's Cancel isn't offered once an item was Submitted (RP-433), so today Rabaed's own cancel (RP-952) is the close where this applies. Left as built until RP-1149 is decided: the answers and Trade, Location and Scopes the holder changed after the last arrival, which a close shares and the Record prints, and a Link the holder removed after it, which the close deletes for everyone.
